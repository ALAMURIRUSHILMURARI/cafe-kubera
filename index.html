Inspired by the lord of treasures, Cafe Kubera is envisioned as a sanctuary of opulence and comfort. Just as Lord Kubera guards the world's wealth, we guard the integrity of every coffee bean we source.

Our Coffee Philosophy

We source single-origin micro-lots from Chikmagalur and Araku Valley, roasting them to perfection to highlight distinct regional notes: from the cardamom-tinted soil spice to the heavy chocolate finish.
